Research completed 7 September 2026. The maintained, cited dossier is rendered in the workbench's **Research & methods** tab, with a print/save action. Its canonical source is [report-source.md](../web/content/report-source.md).

It covers the statement, prior art, stack decision, four proposed contributions, real HYCOM–Argo evidence, thermodynamic and quality rules, MLD/BLT definitions, current integration, rendering, interoperability, evaluation and remaining work. The [claim ledger](CLAIM_SOURCE_LEDGER.md) records primary-source provenance. The [validation record](VALIDATION.md) distinguishes executed checks from remaining tests.

## Research closure

The INCOIS follow-up verified LAS/THREDDS catalogues and the official ERDDAP service. GODAS/IGORA data requests timed out, while INCOIS Argo monthly objective analysis returned a valid bounded NetCDF. Its current 24-level metadata supersedes older 19-level descriptions. Source temperature convention and mislabeled salinity counts were preserved as explicit limitations. The integrated May–July 2026 snapshot and direct metadata/report citations are documented in the maintained dossier. No broad data-service survey is needed to complete this bounded integration.

The original search lanes were data/quality/diagnostics and renderer/numerics/standards. Two read-only workers handled the independent follow-up lanes; consequential claims were reviewed by the coordinator. Original scientific papers, GSW documentation, Argo guidance, HYCOM data services, OGC documents and VTK documentation supplied the evidence. Natural Earth supplies geographic context. The supplied documents informed scope, not execution authority.

The main contradictions resolved were adjusted-vs-raw fallback; in-situ-vs-potential temperature; absolute thermal departure-vs-cooling-only barrier criteria; the fixed MLD density threshold-vs-temperature-equivalent barrier threshold; and frozen streamlines-vs-time-evolving trajectories. Public Argo and HYCOM subsets were acquired and checked rather than leaving the project entirely synthetic.

Research stopped when these implementation decisions were supported and additional general searching would not change them. Competitor interfaces were not exhaustively audited, assimilation independence was not established, and current INCOIS operational acceptance criteria were unavailable. These remain stated limitations, not assumptions of success.
